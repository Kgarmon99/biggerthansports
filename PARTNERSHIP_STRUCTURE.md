# Partnership Strategy: Schools & Events
## Bigger Than Sports Foundation

---

## The Opportunity

**Partner:** [Name] - School/event connector  
**Value:** Access to schools, existing relationships, credibility  
**Your Value:** Professional website, digital presence, event execution

---

## Partnership Models

### Option 1: Revenue Share (Recommended)

**Structure:**
- He brings school/event opportunities
- You handle website, marketing, digital logistics
- Split revenue from sponsored events

**Typical Split:**
| Role | Percentage | Responsibility |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Him** | 20-30% | School relationships, introductions, initial pitch |
| **You** | 70-80% | Website, marketing materials, event digital support, follow-up |

**Example:**
- School pays $2,000 for event partnership
- He gets: $400-600 (20-30%)
- You get: $1,400-1,600 (70-80%)

---

### Option 2: Referral Fee

**Structure:**
- Flat fee per successful school/event secured
- One-time payment when deal closes

**Pricing:**
- Small event (single school): $200-300
- Medium event (district level): $500-750
- Large event (multi-school/citywide): $1,000-1,500

**Pros:** Simple, no ongoing accounting  
**Cons:** Less incentive for long-term relationship building

---

### Option 3: Equity/Advisory Role

**Structure:**
- He becomes official "School Partnerships Advisor"
- Small equity stake or ongoing advisor fee
- Monthly retainer for ongoing relationship management

**Pricing:**
- Monthly retainer: $300-500/month
- Plus: 10-15% of school-sponsored event revenue
- Plus: Title/role on website (builds his credibility too)

---

## What to Offer Him

### Immediate Value:
1. **Website Feature** - "School Partnerships Lead" or "Community Outreach Director"
2. **Professional Materials** - Use the website as proof of legitimacy
3. **Event Support** - You handle all digital/tech aspects
4. **Shared Success** - His connections + your execution = bigger impact

### Long-term Value:
1. **Case Studies** - Document successful school partnerships
2. **Referrals** - He can refer you to other organizations
3. **Portfolio** - Add Bigger Than Sports to his track record
4. **Network** - You introduce him to your contacts too

---

## Pitch to Him

**"Here's the deal:"**

> You have the school relationships. I have the professional digital presence and execution capability. Together we can:
> - Land bigger school partnerships
> - Look more credible to administrators
> - Handle more events without burning out
> - Split the revenue fairly

**His Risk:** Minimal (just introductions)  
**His Reward:** Passive income from his existing network  
**Your Risk:** Time investment  
**Your Reward:** Access to schools you couldn't reach alone

---

## Agreement Structure

### Simple Partnership Agreement Should Include:

1. **Roles & Responsibilities**
   - What he does: Introductions, relationship maintenance
   - What you do: Website, marketing, event execution

2. **Compensation**
   - Percentage or flat fee structure
   - Payment timing (upon contract signing? event completion?)

3. **Exclusivity**
   - Is he your exclusive school partner?
   - Can he work with other nonprofits?

4. **Duration**
   - 6-month trial? 1-year agreement?
   - Easy exit clause if it's not working

5. **Success Metrics**
   - Number of school partnerships
   - Revenue generated
   - Events executed

## Action Plan

### Step 1: The Conversation (This Week)
- Show him the website

### Step 2: Trial Run (Month 1)
- He introduces you to 1-2 schools

- Add more school districts
- Create recurring annual events
- Build case studies for future partners

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Don't:** Give equity without a trial period  
❌ **Don't:** Promise exclusivity before testing the relationship  
❌ **Don't:** Pay upfront - always pay on performance  
❌ **Don't:** Let him control the relationship (you need direct contact eventually)

✅ **Do:** Start small with one school  
✅ **Do:** Get everything in writing  
✅ **Do:** Set clear expectations  
✅ **Do:** Build your own relationships over time

---

## Bottom Line

**He's a door-opener, not a closer.**  
His value is introductions. Your value is execution.

**Fair split:** 20-30% for introductions, 70-80% for everything else.

**Goal:** Use his network to get in, then build direct relationships so you're less dependent over time.
